Welcome to Echo Air Group

We are a virtual airline for people who love aviation, flight simulation and the community around it. Our goal is to combine realistic airline operations with a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere.

From short European hops to long-haul journeys, every flight is another opportunity to explore the world from the cockpit.
