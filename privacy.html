1. Introduction

Echo Air Group respects the privacy of visitors, pilots and other users of the website.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data may be processed when you use the Echo Air Group website and services, why that data is processed, how it may be shared, and what rights you have.

Echo Air Group aims to process only the information that is reasonably necessary to provide and maintain its services.

3. Personal Data We May Process

Depending on how you use the website, Echo Air Group may process the following categories of information:

Data Purpose
Username Used to create and identify your Echo Air Group account.
Password Used to authenticate your account. Passwords are stored as cryptographic password hashes rather than as plain-text passwords.
Display name Used to display your chosen name within the website, including pilot and ranking pages.
NewSky Pilot ID Used to identify your NewSky pilot account and associate your NewSky flight records with your Echo Air Group account.
Flight information Used to calculate flight statistics, stars, rankings, achievements and other pilot information.
Discord User ID Used when you choose to connect your Discord account and for Discord rank-role synchronization.
Account information Used to maintain your account and provide authentication and website functionality.

4. Why We Process Your Data

Echo Air Group may process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Authenticating users.
  • Linking a pilot to their NewSky Pilot ID.
  • Synchronizing flight information.
  • Calculating flight statistics and stars.
  • Maintaining pilot rankings and achievements.
  • Connecting a user's Discord account when requested.
  • Synchronizing Discord rank roles when Discord is linked.
  • Maintaining and securing the website and backend.
  • Responding to support, privacy or legal requests.

5. Legal Bases

Where the GDPR/AVG applies, personal data should only be processed where a valid legal basis exists.

Depending on the specific processing activity, Echo Air Group may rely on a legal basis such as:

  • Performance of a contract or steps requested by you to provide the service.
  • Your consent, where consent is specifically requested.
  • Legitimate interests, where applicable and where those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • Compliance with legal obligations where applicable.

6. Account Passwords

Echo Air Group does not intentionally store account passwords in plain text.

Passwords are processed using a password-hashing function before being stored in the database.

Users should never reuse an important personal password from another service for their Echo Air Group account.

7. NewSky Integration

Echo Air Group can use NewSky information to synchronize flight records for pilots who provide their NewSky Pilot ID.

Depending on the information provided by NewSky, the synchronized information may include flight identifiers, departure and arrival airports, aircraft information, flight duration, distance, rating and flight date/time.

This information is used to provide Echo Air Group's flight statistics, ranking, star and achievement systems.

Echo Air Group does not represent NewSky and is not a replacement for NewSky's own privacy policy or terms.

8. Discord Integration

Discord integration is optional.

If you choose to connect Discord, Echo Air Group may receive and store your Discord user ID so that your Discord account can be associated with your Echo Air Group account.

Echo Air Group may use the Discord user ID to synchronize your Echo Air Group rank with an appropriate Discord role.

Echo Air Group does not need your Discord password to provide this connection.

9. Cookies

Echo Air Group does not intentionally use cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking purposes.

The website is designed to operate without advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

Technical mechanisms used by hosting providers, browsers, authentication infrastructure or third-party services may operate independently of Echo Air Group. Their own policies may therefore apply.

11. Data Storage

Echo Air Group stores application data using a hosted PostgreSQL database provided by Neon.

Data may therefore be processed or stored using infrastructure located outside the Netherlands, depending on the infrastructure and configuration used by the relevant service provider.

Where required by applicable data-protection law, relevant safeguards for international transfers will be used.

12. Data Retention

Echo Air Group keeps personal data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Account information and associated flight information may remain stored while an account is active.

If you request deletion of your account, Echo Air Group will assess the request and delete or anonymize relevant personal data where appropriate and legally possible.
