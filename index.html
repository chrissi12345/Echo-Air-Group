Welcome Aboard

More than an airline. A community.

Whether you are taking your first flight in a simulator or have thousands of hours behind you, Echo Air Group gives you a place to fly, learn, explore and meet other aviation enthusiasts.

✈

Realistic Operations

Structured routes, aircraft assignments, pilot standards and NewSky-based flight logging create a realistic airline environment.

◈

Freedom to Fly

Fly short regional sectors, European routes or long-haul missions. Choose the aircraft and destinations that fit your flying style.

◎

Community First

Echo Air Group is built around pilots. Fly together, participate in events and become part of a growing virtual aviation community.
