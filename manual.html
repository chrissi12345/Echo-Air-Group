05

Flight Operations

Echo Air Group encourages pilots to operate flights in a realistic and professional manner. You do not need to be a real-world airline pilot to enjoy realistic virtual operations.

Before departure

  • Check your aircraft configuration.
  • Verify the departure and arrival airports.
  • Review the planned route.
  • Check the weather and expected conditions.
  • Ensure sufficient fuel is available.
  • Complete an appropriate cockpit setup.

During the flight

  • Follow the planned route where practical.
  • Operate the aircraft safely.
  • Use appropriate navigation and communication procedures.
  • Avoid intentionally unrealistic behaviour.
  • Respect other pilots when flying online.

After landing

  • Complete the flight properly.
  • Shut down or secure the aircraft when appropriate.
  • Ensure your flight data is recorded correctly.
  • Submit the flight through the appropriate system.

Flight Reporting

Echo Air Group uses flight data to track pilot activity, statistics, distance, flight time, ratings and ranking progress.

Flight information

Depending on the available flight data, the system may record information such as:

  • Pilot
  • Departure airport
  • Arrival airport
  • Aircraft
  • Flight duration
  • Flight distance
  • Flight rating
  • Stars earned
  • Flight date and time
Important

Only genuine flights should be submitted. Attempting to artificially increase flight statistics or ranking position is against Echo Air Group standards.

07

Stars & Ranking

Echo Air Group uses a star-based progression system to reward pilots for completing flights, travelling distance and achieving strong flight ratings.

Current star calculation Stars = Flight Minutes + Distance ÷ 10 + Rating

For example, a flight lasting 54 minutes with a distance of 279 km and a rating of 9.87 would produce approximately:

54 + 27.9 + 9.87 = 91.77 Stars

Pilot ranks

Rank 01 Cadet 0 Stars
Rank 02 First Officer 1,000 Stars
Rank 03 Senior First Officer 2,500 Stars
Rank 04 Captain 5,000 Stars
Rank 05 Senior Captain 7,500 Stars
Rank 06 Commander 10,000 Stars

Ranking progression

Your ranking position is based on the number of stars you have earned. Your pilot profile can show your current rank, progress towards the next rank and the amount of stars required to advance.

08

SOP & Realism

Standard Operating Procedures help keep Echo Air Group operations consistent and professional. They are intended as guidance for realistic virtual airline operations.

General operating principles

  • Perform a reasonable pre-flight preparation.
  • Use the correct aircraft configuration.
  • Follow appropriate departure procedures.
  • Maintain reasonable navigation accuracy.
  • Use realistic speeds and altitudes.
  • Perform an appropriate approach and landing.
  • Complete the flight rather than intentionally ending it early.
Realism over perfection

Echo Air Group is a virtual airline, not a professional aviation examination. Small mistakes happen. The purpose of our SOP is to encourage good habits and realistic flying, not to make every flight stressful.

11

Pilot Conduct

Every Echo Air Group pilot contributes to the atmosphere of the airline. We expect members to act responsibly and respectfully.

Expected behaviour

  • Respect other pilots and community members.
  • Follow staff instructions when necessary.
  • Use the airline systems honestly.
  • Submit only legitimate flight information.
  • Avoid harassment, discrimination or intentionally disruptive behaviour.
  • Represent Echo Air Group professionally.

Statistics and ranking integrity

Echo Air Group's ranking system exists to reward genuine activity. Manipulating flight data, intentionally submitting fake flights or otherwise attempting to gain an unfair advantage can result in administrative action.
