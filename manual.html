Welcome to Echo Air Group

Welcome to Echo Air Group, a virtual airline created for aviation and flight simulation enthusiasts. Whether you are an experienced virtual pilot or just starting your journey, we are happy to have you with us.

Echo Air Group is built around realistic operations, enjoyable flying and an active community. Our goal is not simply to collect flight hours, but to create an environment where pilots can improve their skills and enjoy aviation together.

This Pilot Manual explains the main rules, procedures and expectations that apply to every Echo Air Group pilot. Please read it carefully before operating your first official flight.

This manual may be updated when Echo Air Group changes its operations or community policies. Important changes will be communicated through the official Discord server.

2

About Echo Air Group

Echo Air Group is a fictional virtual airline created for flight simulation. The airline exists entirely within the virtual aviation community and has no connection to any real-world airline.

Our primary callsign is ECV, and our pilots operate flights using the Echo Air Group network and fleet.

Flight activity and pilot statistics are managed through NewSky, allowing pilots to track their flights, flight time, ratings and progression within the airline.

Echo Air Group welcomes pilots of different experience levels. New pilots can start at the Cadet rank and work their way through the airline's rank system as they complete flights and earn stars.

Pilot Ranks

Echo Air Group uses a progression system based on stars. Every completed flight can contribute to your total star count. As you accumulate stars, your pilot rank automatically progresses.

Rank Required Stars Description
Cadet 0 Starting rank for new pilots.
First Officer 1,000 Established Echo Air Group pilot.
Senior First Officer 2,500 Experienced First Officer.
Captain 5,000 Experienced airline captain.
Senior Captain 7,500 Highly experienced Echo Air Group pilot.
Commander 10,000 Highest standard pilot rank.

Rank progression is based on accumulated stars and is separate from real-world pilot licences or qualifications.

Fleet & Aircraft

Echo Air Group operates a mixed fleet covering regional, narrow-body and wide-body operations. Pilots should always select an aircraft that is appropriate for the planned route.

Category Aircraft Typical Use
REGIONAL Embraer E190 Short and medium-haul operations
NARROWBODY A319 / A320 / A320neo / A321neo Short and medium-haul operations
NARROWBODY B737-700 / B737-800 / B737 MAX Short and medium-haul operations
WIDEBODY A350-900 Long-haul operations

Aircraft Selection

Pilots are expected to use realistic aircraft for the route being flown. A long-haul route should not be operated with an aircraft that does not have the required range, while short regional routes should generally use suitable short-haul equipment.

Pilots should also ensure that the aircraft being used is appropriate for the departure and arrival airports.

Flight Operations

Echo Air Group flights should be conducted as realistically as reasonably possible. Pilots are encouraged to follow real-world airline procedures and normal operating practices.

Before the Flight

  1. Select a valid Echo Air Group route.
  2. Choose an appropriate aircraft.
  3. Create a realistic flight plan.
  4. Check the expected flight time and route.
  5. Review weather and airport information.
  6. Prepare the aircraft using normal procedures.
  7. Use the correct Echo Air Group callsign and flight number.

During the Flight

  • Operate the aircraft realistically.
  • Follow ATC instructions when connected to an online network.
  • Use appropriate speeds, altitudes and procedures.
  • Avoid unrealistic manoeuvres.
  • Keep NewSky tracking active for the official flight.

After the Flight

  • Complete the flight normally.
  • Park and shut down the aircraft correctly.
  • Allow NewSky to finish recording the flight.
  • Check that the flight has been logged correctly.

Pilot Responsibilities

Every Echo Air Group pilot represents the airline whenever operating an official flight. Pilots are therefore expected to act responsibly and respectfully.

  • Use the correct Echo Air Group callsign: ECV + flight number.
  • Log official flights through NewSky.
  • Use an appropriate aircraft for the route.
  • Follow online network rules when using services such as VATSIM or IVAO.
  • Respect ATC controllers and other pilots.
  • Do not intentionally manipulate flight statistics.
  • Report technical problems to Echo Air Group staff.
  • Keep your pilot information up to date.

NewSky & Flight Logging

NewSky is used by Echo Air Group to track official flights and pilot progression.

Flights should be connected to the correct pilot account and operated using the appropriate Echo Air Group flight information.

Official Flight Requirements

  • The flight must be operated under the Echo Air Group callsign.
  • The flight must use a valid Echo Air Group route.
  • NewSky tracking should remain active during the flight.
  • The flight should be completed normally.
  • Pilots must not intentionally manipulate flight data.

If a flight does not appear correctly in NewSky, contact staff rather than attempting to manipulate or recreate the flight statistics.

Stars & Flight Rating

Echo Air Group uses a star-based progression system. Stars are calculated from flight performance and the flight itself, allowing pilots to progress through the ranks.

Star Calculation

The current Echo Air Group ranking system uses flight duration, distance and flight rating when calculating the number of stars earned.

Stars = Flight Minutes + (Distance ÷ 10) + Rating

Flight Rating

Flight ratings are used to represent the quality of a completed flight. A good rating contributes positively toward pilot progression.

Ranking

Your total stars are displayed on the Echo Air Group ranking system. When you reach the required number of stars for the next rank, your rank can be updated automatically by the airline's ranking system.

Activity & Inactivity Policy

Echo Air Group understands that pilots have school, work, family and other responsibilities. Real life always comes first.

Active Pilot

Pilots who regularly complete flights and participate in the community remain active members of Echo Air Group.

30-Day Rule

Pilots who have not completed a flight for 30 days may be removed from the active roster in accordance with the Echo Air Group activity policy.

Leave of Absence

If you know that you will be unable to fly for an extended period, contact the Echo Air Group staff through Discord. Staff may take the circumstances into account before applying the inactivity policy.

The purpose of the activity policy is to keep the airline active and maintain an engaged pilot roster — not to punish pilots for having real-life responsibilities.

Events & Group Flights

Echo Air Group regularly encourages pilots to participate in group flights, tours and special events.

Group Flight Standards

  • Use the assigned flight number when applicable.
  • Use the aircraft specified for the event when required.

Safety & Flight Standards

Echo Air Group does not require every pilot to operate at airline-training-simulator standards. However, pilots are expected to make a reasonable effort to operate realistically.

Recommended Standards

  • Use realistic flight plans.
  • Use realistic cruise altitudes.
  • Respect aircraft performance limitations.
  • Use realistic takeoff and landing procedures.
  • Avoid unnecessary extreme manoeuvres.
  • Follow normal aircraft procedures.
  • Respect online ATC instructions.

Unacceptable Flight Behaviour

  • Intentionally crashing aircraft to manipulate statistics.
  • Using impossible or unrealistic flight times.
  • Intentionally manipulating NewSky data.
  • Using an inappropriate aircraft simply to complete a route.
  • Deliberately disrupting other pilots or ATC.
14

Disciplinary Actions

Echo Air Group aims to resolve problems fairly and professionally. However, serious or repeated violations may result in disciplinary action.

Possible Actions

  • Informal warning.
  • Official warning.
  • Temporary restriction from events or airline activities.
  • Suspension from the airline.
  • Removal from the pilot roster.

The action taken depends on the seriousness of the incident, the pilot's history and the circumstances surrounding the situation.

Staff decisions are intended to protect the airline and its community. Pilots are encouraged to contact staff if they believe a decision was made incorrectly.

  • Echo Air Group is a fictional virtual airline created exclusively for flight simulation.
