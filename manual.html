Pilot Manual

Fly the Echo way.

A quick-reference guide for pilots. Expand this page later with your official SOPs and rank requirements.

01 · Before Flight

  • Check aircraft, route and flight plan.
  • Verify departure, arrival and alternate airports.
  • Check weather and simulator setup.
  • Book the correct NewSky flight.

02 · During Flight

  • Operate within aircraft limitations.
  • Use appropriate lights and configuration.
  • Maintain stable approaches.
  • Follow VATSIM/IVAO rules when online.

03 · NewSky

  • Start the flight through NewSky.
  • Fly the booked aircraft and route accurately.
  • End the flight after parking.
  • Review your post-flight rating.

04 · Community

  • Be respectful and helpful.
  • Ask for help when needed.
  • Join events and group flights.
  • Represent Echo professionally.
