Welcome to Echo Air Group

Welcome to Echo Air Group — a virtual airline built for flight simulation enthusiasts who want to combine realistic operations with a friendly and enjoyable community.

Whether you are completely new to virtual aviation or an experienced simmer, this manual is your reference for operating as an Echo Air Group pilot.

Our goal is simple: fly realistically, have fun, and improve together.

This manual is a living document. Echo Air Group may update procedures, requirements, or policies when necessary. Pilots are responsible for staying informed about important changes.

04

Pilot Requirements

Echo Air Group welcomes pilots with different levels of experience. You do not need to be an expert pilot to join, but every member is expected to learn and follow the basic standards of the group.

Minimum Requirements

  • Pilots must be at least 14 years old.
  • You must own and be able to operate one of our supported flight simulators.
  • You must have a NewSky account for flight logging.
  • You must provide accurate information when registering with Echo Air Group.
  • You must agree to follow this Pilot Manual.
  • You must treat other pilots, staff, and online aviation communities with respect.

Experience is not a requirement. If you are new to flight simulation, staff and other pilots can help you learn the systems and procedures.

Flight Operations

Every official Echo Air Group flight should be conducted as a realistic airline operation from preparation through shutdown.

Before Flight

01

Select Your Flight

Choose an appropriate Echo Air Group route and verify the departure, destination, aircraft, and flight number.

02

Prepare the Aircraft

Complete the appropriate pre-flight preparation for your aircraft.

03

Prepare the Flight Plan

File a realistic route and use suitable departure, arrival, altitude, and navigation procedures.

04

Connect to NewSky

Start the NewSky flight tracking system before beginning the official flight.

During Flight

  • Fly the aircraft safely and realistically.
  • Follow ATC instructions when connected to an online network.
  • Maintain a realistic cruise altitude and speed.
  • Avoid unrealistic maneuvers or deliberate statistical manipulation.
  • Monitor the aircraft throughout the flight.

After Flight

  • Complete the arrival and shutdown procedures.
  • Ensure the flight has been successfully submitted through NewSky.
  • Check that the recorded flight information is correct.
07

NewSky & Flight Logging

NewSky is the flight tracking and logging system used by Echo Air Group. Official flights should be submitted through NewSky so that flight statistics, rankings, and pilot progress can be maintained.

Flight Logging Requirements

  • Start NewSky before beginning an official flight.
  • Use your own Echo Air Group pilot account.
  • Ensure the correct aircraft and flight are selected.
  • Complete the flight normally before submitting it.
  • Do not intentionally manipulate flight data.
  • Check your flight after submission if possible.

If NewSky fails to record a flight correctly, contact Echo Air Group staff rather than attempting to modify or fabricate flight statistics.

Flight Statistics

Echo Air Group uses flight data to calculate pilot statistics and progression. Depending on the current system configuration, statistics may include flight count, flight time, distance, rating, stars, rank, and other performance information.

08

Pilot Responsibilities

Being an Echo Air Group pilot means representing the airline whenever you operate under our identity. Every pilot is expected to act responsibly.

  • Represent Echo Air Group professionally.
  • Use the correct Echo Air Group callsign and flight identification when applicable.
  • Follow applicable simulator, network, and community rules.
  • Respect ATC controllers and other pilots.
  • Keep your Echo Air Group profile information accurate.
  • Report technical problems or operational issues to staff.
  • Do not intentionally exploit bugs or systems to gain rankings or statistics.
  • Help maintain a positive community environment.

Your actions as a pilot reflect on the entire Echo Air Group community. Professionalism, respect, and good judgment are always expected.

09

Flight Standards

Echo Air Group does not expect every pilot to operate at airline-training level. However, pilots should continuously work toward realistic and competent flight operations.

Recommended Standards

  • Perform a reasonable pre-flight briefing.
  • Use realistic flight planning.
  • Use appropriate speeds and altitudes.
  • Use proper takeoff and landing procedures.
  • Avoid extreme bank angles during normal airline operations.
  • Avoid unrealistic acceleration, braking, or maneuvering.
  • Follow appropriate aircraft procedures.

Online Networks

When flying on an online network such as IVAO or another supported aviation network, pilots are expected to follow the rules of that network.

ATC instructions should be followed unless doing so would create a safety issue. Pilots should use appropriate aviation phraseology and maintain professional behavior.

Dangerous or deliberately disruptive behavior on online networks is not acceptable and may result in disciplinary action from Echo Air Group in addition to any action taken by the network.

10

Activity & Inactivity Policy

Echo Air Group is designed to be an active community, but we understand that pilots have school, work, family, and other responsibilities.

Active Pilot

Pilots who regularly complete flights and participate in the community are considered active members.

Temporary Inactivity

Short periods without flying are acceptable. Real life always takes priority over virtual aviation.

Extended Absence

Pilots expecting a longer absence are encouraged to inform staff so their account can remain in good standing.

Abandoned Accounts

Accounts that remain inactive for an extended period without communication may be reviewed by staff.

Leave of Absence

If you know that you will be unavailable for an extended period, contact Echo Air Group staff before the absence whenever possible. Staff can record the absence and prevent unnecessary account action.

Rank progression is intended to reward consistent participation and successful flight operations. Rank should never be treated as a reason to compromise realistic or safe flying.

Progression

Pilot statistics can include total flights, flight time, distance, ratings, stars, current rank, and progress toward the next rank. These statistics are tracked through the Echo Air Group flight system.

12

Pilot Conduct

Echo Air Group is a community. Every member is responsible for helping maintain a respectful and enjoyable environment.

Expected Behavior

  • Treat other members with respect.
  • Respect staff decisions and operational guidance.
  • Avoid unnecessary arguments and personal attacks.
  • Keep community discussions appropriate.
  • Respect the rules of external networks and platforms used by Echo Air Group.
  • Report serious problems to staff instead of escalating conflicts publicly.

Prohibited Behavior

  • Harassment or targeted abuse.
  • Deliberate disruption of online aviation networks.
  • Deliberate manipulation of flight statistics.
  • Impersonating Echo Air Group staff.
  • Using unauthorized methods to gain access to accounts or systems.
  • Behavior that seriously damages the reputation or operation of Echo Air Group.

Disciplinary Action

Depending on the severity of an incident, staff may issue a warning, restrict certain privileges, temporarily suspend an account, or permanently remove a pilot from Echo Air Group.

Staff will consider the circumstances of each case. Serious violations may result in immediate action.

13

Final Provisions

By joining and remaining a member of Echo Air Group, pilots agree to follow this Pilot Manual and any additional official rules published by the group.

  • Echo Air Group reserves the right to update this manual when necessary.
  • Important policy changes may be communicated through official community channels.
  • Staff decisions may be made when situations are not explicitly covered by this manual.
  • Pilots are responsible for staying informed about relevant rules and updates.
  • Echo Air Group is a virtual airline created for flight simulation purposes.
  • Virtual airline statistics, ranks, and achievements have no real-world aviation authority.

When you are unsure about something, ask. The purpose of this manual is to provide guidance, not to make virtual aviation unnecessarily complicated.
